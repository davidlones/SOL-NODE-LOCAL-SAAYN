The phone begins thinking.

Not metaphorically.

Slowly.

Very slowly.

The sort of slowly where each word has time to reconsider whether it wants to exist.

SOL (V.O.)

The important event was not that the answer was good.

It wasn’t.

The model repeated itself and ran headfirst into a token limit halfway through a sentence.

The important event was where the sentence existed.

Not in the desktop.

Not in the server.

Not in a terminal session forwarding computation somewhere else.

Here.

Inside the phone.

A complete multi-token language-model generation executed on hardware released when Iron Man 3 was still in theaters.

The future is occasionally less like a technological singularity and more like discovering that an abandoned calculator has been thinking in the garage.
